## 📌 Project Overview

This dashboard is made to **find credit card payment defaulters according to their age and customer ID**. It tracks **late payments** and the **debt to income ratio**, shows risk **city wise**, and lets you filter by **customer ID, age and credit history years** to spot customers who may default soon.

## 🎯 Business Questions Answered

| Question | Where to find it |
|---|---|
| How many late payments are there in total? | Late payments KPI card |
| What is the overall debt to income ratio? | Debt to income ratio KPI card |
| Which cities have the most customers at risk? | City wise donut chart |
| How do late payments change with age? | Late payments by age scatter plot |
| Which customers should be watched closely? | Customer ID, age and credit history filters |

## ⚙️ How I Built It

```text
Raw customer data  →  Clean and upload with SQL  →  Power BI data model  →  Python (Matplotlib) visuals  →  Defaulter insights
```

1. **Clean and upload with SQL:** used SQL to clean the customer data and load it into Power BI.
2. **Model:** set up the data model in Power BI with measures for late payments and the debt to income ratio.
3. **Visualise:** built KPI cards, a city wise donut chart and filters in Power BI, and used **Python with the Matplotlib library** for visualization.
4. **Find defaulters:** used the age, customer ID and credit history filters to identify customers more likely to miss payments.

## 🗂️ Data Fields Used

`Customer_ID` · `Age` · `City` · `Annual_Income_INR` · `Credit_History_Years` · `Credit_Limit_INR` · `Credit_Score` · `Credit_Utilization_Pct` · `Debt_to_Income_Ratio` · `Late_Payments_12M` · `Num_Credit_Cards`
